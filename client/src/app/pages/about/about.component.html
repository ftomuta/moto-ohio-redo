About the Program

Ohio's official motorcycle rider education program — making roads safer since 1992.

Our Mission

Saving Lives Through Education

Motorcycle Ohio is the official rider training program of the Ohio Department of Public Safety (ODPS). We exist to reduce motorcycle fatalities and serious injuries on Ohio roads by providing expert, evidence-based training to new and experienced riders alike.

Founded in 1992 as part of the national MSF-affiliated network, Motorcycle Ohio has trained more than 200,000 Ohioans and operates at over 80 training sites across all 88 counties.

All curriculum is based on the MSF's nationally‑recognized standards and is updated regularly to incorporate the latest research in rider safety and adult learning.

An Official ODPS Program

Motorcycle Ohio is administered by the Ohio Department of Public Safety's Division of Emergency Management and operates under Ohio Revised Code §4508.08. All program fees are used exclusively to fund training operations.
