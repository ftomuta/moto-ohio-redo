The Path

Your Certification Journey

From application to your first class, most candidates complete certification in 3–4 months.

1

Meet the Prerequisites

Must hold a valid Ohio motorcycle endorsement (M or M3) for at least 2 years with no major violations. Must be 21 or older at time of application.

2

Attend an Info Session

Attend a free 2-hour open house hosted by a Regional Coordinator in your area. Hear from current instructors and get all your questions answered.

3

Complete the IPC

The Instructor Preparation Course (IPC) is a 5-day intensive that covers MSF curriculum, adult learning theory, and range coaching techniques.

4

Pass Certification Evaluations

Demonstrate your coaching skills in a mock BRC delivery evaluated by a Regional Coordinator. Achieve passing scores on both classroom and range modules.

5

Deliver Your First Course

Congratulations — you're certified! Partner with your regional training site to get on the schedule and start teaching alongside a mentor instructor.

Requirements

Before You Apply

📄

Valid OH Motorcycle Endorsement

Active M or M3 endorsement for 2+ continuous years prior to IPC start date.

🎂

Age 21 or Older

Must be at least 21 years old at the time of IPC enrollment.

🚦

Clean Driving Record

No major violations (OVI, reckless) within the past 3 years.

🏍️

Own or Access a Motorcycle

Must provide a street-legal motorcycle for use during the IPC range days.

🦺

Full Protective Gear

Helmet, jacket, gloves, over-the-ankle footwear, and long pants required for all range sessions.

📝

Completed Application

Submit the Instructor Candidate Application Form to your regional coordinator prior to the IPC.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

The IPC is offered at no cost to qualified candidates. ODPS covers the curriculum, range time, and instructor fees as part of the program's mission to grow the instructor pool statewide.

There is no strict minimum, but certified instructors are expected to maintain proficiency by teaching at least 4 courses per year. Full-time and part-time schedules are both accommodated based on site availability.

Instructors must complete a 1-day annual recertification update and maintain their valid motorcycle endorsement. Regional Coordinators schedule update training throughout the spring each year.

Once certified, you may volunteer or contract with any approved MSF training site in Ohio. Many instructors teach at multiple locations to earn additional income during peak spring/summer season.

IPC sessions are typically held in March, June, and September at rotating locations across the state. Contact us or your nearest Regional Coordinator to be put on the notification list for the next open IPC.
