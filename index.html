Why take a course

Waive the Riding Test

Complete a BRC and skip the BMV road skills test entirely — faster path to your license.

Certified Instructors

All coaches are ODPS‑certified with hundreds of hours of rider education experience.

Insurance Discounts

Choose Your Course

From first-time riders to veterans, we have a course designed for every skill level.

Beginner

Basic RiderCourse (BRC)

The gold standard for new riders. Classroom instruction + 10 hours of range exercises. Completion waives the BMV skills test.

⏱ 2 days 📋 No experience needed

Advanced RiderCourse (ARC)

Challenge your existing skills with higher-speed exercises, emergency stops, and swerving techniques on your own bike.
